I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Wayne State University, advised by Dr. Suzan Arslanturk. My research focuses on machine learning, explainable artificial intelligence, and statistical genomics for cancer biomarker discovery.

My doctoral work develops methods for analyzing high-dimensional, sparse somatic variant and gene-expression data, identifying biomarkers associated with aggressive cancer, and connecting computational predictions to biological pathways and potential therapeutic strategies.

I am also an educator with experience teaching object-oriented programming and mentoring undergraduate capstone teams. I value clear explanations, practical examples, and research-informed teaching.

Research

My research bridges artificial intelligence and computational biology, with a focus on methods that make cancer-related molecular data more interpretable and clinically meaningful.

CLOVER: Cross-Cancer Learning for Somatic Variant Biomarkers

A deep-learning framework leveraging somatic mutation data across five cancer types to address limited aggressive prostate cancer samples and recognize cancer-specific and shared biomarkers.

Deep LearningCancer Genomics   ACM-BCB 2026 ↗

Variant-Level Biomarkers of Aggressive Prostate Cancer

Developed a hypothesis-testing approach to identify variants that differentiate aggressive and indolent prostate cancer. Identified four significant variants and evaluated their biological pathway associations.

Statistical GenomicsBiomarker Discovery

Graph Representation Learning for Sparse Somatic Mutation Data

Combines patient-similarity graphs, variational graph autoencoders, contrastive learning, and graph-based explainability to identify informative representations and influential variants.

Graph Neural NetworksUnder Review

Biomarker-Driven Drug Repurposing

Uses gene expression, mutation-defined patient subgroups, and similarity-based ranking to prioritize drug candidates for biologically related breast and prostate cancers.

Drug RepurposingMulti-omics

Network-Based Bioinformatics

Studied shared disease mechanisms through gene-expression analysis, protein–protein interaction and gene-regulatory networks, and pathway analysis.

Systems BiologyNetwork Analysis

Future research directions

My planned work includes a generative AI assistant for variant interpretation, multi-task learning for single-cell prostate cancer analysis, multimodal DNA–RNA–protein foundation models, treatment-resistance modeling, and federated biomarker discovery.
